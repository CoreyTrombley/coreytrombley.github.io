Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: corey.trombley33@gmail.com. I read everything and usually reply within a few days.

Where is my save?

Your progress is stored only on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so uninstalling the game erases it.

How do I back up or move my progress?

Open the MORE tab, find the save card, and tap EXPORT SAVE CODE. Keep that code somewhere safe (a note or an email to yourself). On a new install, paste it in the same card and tap IMPORT.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. JUNKRUNNER works fully offline. Your Yard keeps earning while you are away, based on your device clock.

Something looks wrong

Email me with your device model, the game version (shown on the title screen) and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

What I collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and I never receive any information about you or your device.
